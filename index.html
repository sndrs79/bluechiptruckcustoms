Built before boring

GMC called this generation Blue Chip. These two still are.

Conroe, Texas, near Houston

Blue Chip Truck Customs is Mike Sanders and two classic GMC trucks in Conroe, Texas, near Houston. He builds only for himself. The trucks are his, and the business offers appearances and rentals.

A 1956 GMC 100 and a 1958 GMC 100, both driver-ready, both owner-driven. GMC Blue Chip is the right name for the pair: light 100-series pickups from the years GMC used that standard, kept in Conroe, Texas, near Houston.

A show, a cruise-in, or a photo shoot is an appearance. A film or a commercial is a rental, and those jobs go through USA Movie Cars. Mike drives. The truck comes home the same day.

Ophelia

1956 GMC 100 (Ophelia)

Ophelia is orange. The visor is molded in. The lights are round. The bed is a Fleetside with a stainless spear, and the tailgate carries a GMC script made for this truck. She is a 1956 GMC 100, and she is the truck people ask for when the job needs the earlier face: a single pair of round lamps, a visor that was stamped into the roof, and a bed that still reads as a working pickup.

She leaves from Conroe, Texas, near Houston. Half a day on site is four hours. A full day is eight. The rate is on the booking card below, per truck. If the call is a production, use the 1956 listing at USA Movie Cars and the desk at 602-882-2705.

The 58

1958 GMC 100

The 58 is blue, with a white roof and a white bed rail. Quad headlights, a GMC grille, and a white front bumper with chrome guards. She is a 1958 GMC 100, two years further into the same Blue Chip run, with the four-lamp face that marks the later trucks.

Same home, same rules. Appearances and rentals only. Mike built her for himself, and he is the one who brings her to the job. Photo shoots and cruise-ins book on the day rate. Film and commercial rentals stay on the USA Movie Cars listing for the 1958.
