Blue Chip Truck Customs books appearances and rentals from Conroe, Texas, near Houston. The trucks are a 1956 GMC 100, Ophelia, and a 1958 GMC 100, The 58. Mike Sanders built them for himself, and he drives them.

Shows, cruise-ins, and photo shoots use the day rate. A half day is four hours on site at $750. A full day is eight hours at $1,200. The price is per truck. Call or text (936) 657-0123, or write the truck, the date, and the place.

Film and commercial rentals go through USA Movie Cars. The trucks stay owner-driven. The 1956 and the 1958 each have a listing. Their desk is 602-882-2705.
