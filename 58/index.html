The 58

1958 GMC 100

The 58 is the 1958 GMC 100 Mike Sanders keeps in Conroe, Texas, near Houston. She is blue, with a white roof and a white bed rail. Quad headlights, a GMC grille, and a white front bumper with chrome guards.

He built her for himself. Blue Chip Truck Customs offers appearances and rentals of his trucks. A show, a cruise-in, or a photo shoot books as an appearance. A film or a commercial goes through USA Movie Cars. Mike drives. The truck is back in Conroe, Texas, near Houston the same day.

She is the later face of the same run. GMC Blue Chip still fits: a light 100-series pickup, four lamps across the front, and a driver who owns her. Two years on from Ophelia, the roof and the bed rail went white and the lamps went to quads.

A half day is four hours on site at $750. A full day is eight hours at $1,200, for this truck. Write mike@bluechiptruckcustoms.com with the date and the place. For a production, use the 1958 GMC 100 listing at USA Movie Cars, 602-882-2705.
